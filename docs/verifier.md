# The Verifier Role

The Verifier is the agent asked to establish what the evidence supports. A business analyst or product owner can use it to check an acceptance rule. An architect, engineer, QC engineer, or PM can use it to review a change or prepare a release decision.

A useful handoff answers four questions.

| Question | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| What had to be true? | The rule, expected outcome, and scope. |
| What did we check? | The scenario, data, environment, and exact assertion or evidence. |
| What happened? | The observed result and the cause if it was verified. |
| What remains? | The gap, unresolved assumption, or next check. |

The person responsible for the work confirms expected behavior and makes the final decision. See [the reward rename example](samples.md#sir-lump-gets-a-promotion) for a handoff that shows why two assertions matter.
